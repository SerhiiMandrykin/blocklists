## Summary

Add malicious domains from CERT-UA#6318863 (Соціальна інженерія у виконанні UAC-0145: компрометація у процесі працевлаштування / Social engineering by UAC-0145: compromise during the job recruitment process).

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Threat actor** | UAC-0145 (subcluster of UAC-0002, aka Sandworm / APT44 / Seashell Blizzard) |
| **Campaign** | Fake job-recruitment social engineering targeting Ukrainian IT specialists/sysadmins: attackers pose as an IT company ("ATLAS Business Group") hiring for a fake "Sopra Steria Bulgaria" project, conduct real Zoom interviews, then deliver a trojanized WireGuard VPN client ("SopraVPN") that decrypts and executes hidden PowerShell/Linux payloads via a covert `SymmetricKey` config option. Active since at least May 2026. |
| **Domains added** | 4 |
| **Source** | https://cert.gov.ua/article/6318863 |

## Domains to block

- `douncloud.site` — payload delivery / C2 (`/sitedatastorageadvanced/` URL with victim UUID + MachineGuid)
- `soprasteria-bg.com` — fake "Sopra Steria Bulgaria" website impersonating soprasteria.com; also used as sender email domain (registered 2026-07-16)
- `soprasteriabg.com` — related attacker-registered typosquat (registered 2026-05-14)
- `atlasgroup-ua.com` — fake "ATLAS Business Group" IT company website (registered 2026-02-10)

## Excluded (legitimate services abused by attackers)

- `sourceforge.net` — legitimate open-source hosting platform; attackers hosted the trojanized "SopraVPN" client in SourceForge projects (`soprabulgariavpn`, `sopravpn`)
- `ukr.net` — legitimate Ukrainian email provider; used for attacker mailbox `alex.boichenkoit@ukr.net`
- `soprasteria.com`, `soprasteria.bg` — official domains of the real Sopra Steria company being impersonated
- `files.sopra.local` — non-routable `.local` internal hostname resolved via the attacker-controlled VPN DNS server; not blockable/meaningful at public DNS level

## Notes

- The trojanized VPN client is built from WireGuard source code with a modified config parser: a non-standard `SymmetricKey` option carries AES-256-GCM-encrypted PowerShell (Windows) or cURL download commands (Linux), decrypted with a key derived from `PrivateKey` and executed via WireGuard's native `runScriptCommand` (`PostUp`) mechanism. `PrivateKey`/`PublicKey` use a custom BASE64 alphabet shuffled with Fisher–Yates seeded by CRC32 of the `SymmetricKey` value.
- The Linux variant downloads its payload through the VPN tunnel itself (attacker DNS server at 139.28.36.23), so DNS blocklists mitigate the initial-stage delivery (`douncloud.site`) and phishing infrastructure, but network-level blocking of `139.28.36.23` (UDP/51820) is also recommended.
- CERT-UA recommends allowing access to corporate resources only from managed devices with EDR — especially relevant for telecom operators and IT companies whose staff are the campaign's targets.
